01 / OPEN ROLE

The work is here. We need the woodworker.

Now hiring Mooresville, NC Full time · hands-on lead

Working Shop Lead — Commercial Casework

We already have trusted CNC operation and separate engineering support. We need the experienced woodworker who makes sure the work actually goes together.

This is a hands-on lead role. You’ll receive approved drawings, CNC-cut parts, purchased materials, and a required date. You’ll review the work for constructability, plan secondary operations, lead assembly, solve difficult details, and take the job to shop-ready completion — while teaching the people working beside you.

You probably fit if:

  • You have five or more years building casework, fixtures, production cabinetry, or architectural millwork.
  • You understand particleboard, MDF, melamine, laminate, veneer, edge banding, hardware, and frameless assembly.
  • You can read a drawing and see the build sequence — and the likely failure points — before anything is cut.
  • You enjoy solving difficult secondary operations and teaching someone else to do them correctly.
  • You still want to build, not only supervise.

What you won’t be asked to own: sales, estimating, CAD detailing, G-code, or corporate plant management. The owner keeps customers and pricing. Engineering keeps Mozaik and CNC-ready data. Your job is how approved work actually gets built.

02 / HOW WE HIRE

Two tracks. One standard.

We hire experienced hands who are still getting better, and green builders who learn fast. We pay above market to get and keep both.

Track 01 · Experienced hands

Mastered a craft. Still curious.

People who’ve done the work for years, will teach what they know, and stay curious about what they don’t — better systems, better tools, and a say in building them.

Bring your experience. Leave “the way it’s always been done.”

Track 02 · Green builders

No trade background. Sharp and coachable.

People we train across engineering, fabrication, and installation. As the company adds capabilities, early people get first shot at leading them. Not hiring for this track just yet — but introduce yourself.

No experience required. High standards required.

At any level of experience What we don’t hire: “this is the way I’ve always done it.” We look for aptitude, attitude, and coachability — your craft gets you the interview; your curiosity gets you the job.
