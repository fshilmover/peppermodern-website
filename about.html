About · Founded 2025 · Mooresville, NC

Skilled work deserves a well-run company.

Peppermodern started with a simple observation: the trades don’t have a talent problem, they have an operations problem.

01 / THE COMPANY

A trade company built like a product company.

Peppermodern is a millwork and casework company in Mooresville, North Carolina, built for general contractors — and for owners and designers who want the same clarity.

Our shop runs on a single digital thread: the model that prices the job is the model that draws the submittal is the file that runs the CNC. Bids are engineered, not guessed. Parts come off the machine labeled and sequenced for assembly. Schedules run from a complete release package to delivery — we define the clock before we quote the date.

We use technology where it helps the work move faster and more accurately. It doesn’t replace judgment, skilled hands, or a straight conversation.
